Where is the center?

  1. Click Start. The screen goes fullscreen.
  2. A symbol appears at a random place. Click where you think its center is.
  3. Click once; you cannot undo it. The next symbol appears right away.
  4. Do not use any aids (ruler, finger, tracing the outline). Use a mouse or trackpad and do not zoom.
  5. There are 2 parts (15 symbols in total, about 2 minutes). There is a short break screen between them.
